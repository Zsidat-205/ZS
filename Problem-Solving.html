Restaurant Digital Transformation Project

As part of my interest in web development and digital transformation, I developed a concept for a modern steakhouse called Sidat's Steakhouse acting as the Chief Technology Officer (CTO).

The purpose of the website is to improve customer engagement, increase operational efficiency and enhance the overall dining experience through digital technology.

The project demonstrates how web-based solutions can support business growth, improve communication and provide convenient online services for customers.

Sidat's Steakhouse Digital Solution

Sidat's Steakhouse specialises in premium steaks, grilled dishes and burgers. The website has been designed to provide a modern and engaging customer experience.

Benefits of the Website

  • Improves customer engagement through online interaction.
  • Increases operational efficiency through automated reservations.
  • Enhances customer experience with quick access to information.
  • Supports communication between customers and the restaurant.
  • Promotes special offers and events online.
  • Provides accessibility and a mobile-friendly design.
  • Encourages customer retention through loyalty rewards.

This project demonstrates how technology can transform traditional businesses through innovative web solutions and reflects my interests in digital transformation, business technology and data-driven decision making.

Business Context & Strategy

This webpage demonstrates how web development can support a restaurant's digital transformation strategy. The aim is to improve customer engagement, simplify booking requests, collect customer feedback, and strengthen the restaurant's online presence through integrated digital solutions.
